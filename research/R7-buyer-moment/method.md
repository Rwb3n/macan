# R7: what a buyer thinks when they land, and what they already know how to use (exploration, 2026-10-24, before any read)

Principal, 2026-10-24: "the buyer side is sorely lacking. it looks like coding genius that has no idea what another living person thinks about when going on online" (D17).

**This is exploration, not a screen.** There is no decision rule. It produces:
- a read-back of the buyer's pages, screen by screen, for the principal to approve;
- one guess with its test (a new Q).

The real test is people using it (a later study, run by the principal).

## Sources, fixed now
1. **The buyer's moment and questions.**
   - **Read:** `research/R2-gaps/evidence/gap-buyer.md` (already on disk; every point there has a URL).
   - **Output:** the questions in the order a person asks them, each with its R2 quote and URL.
2. **What tested UK patterns say.** Four GOV.UK Design System or Service Manual pages, one scrape each:
   - the start page pattern;
   - "one thing per page" (structuring forms);
   - question pages;
   - "check answers".

   Record each page's rule in its own words, with the URL.
3. **What "normal" looks like.** The opening screen of checkatrade.com, mybuilder.com and bark.com, one scrape each. For each, record:
   - the first thing asked;
   - what it says about trust;
   - what it says about cost;
   - how many questions per screen, if the first step shows it.

## Limits, stated before the read
- **No buyer has been watched using anything.** This tells us what people say and what tested patterns are; it does not prove our pages will work.
- **The quote sites sell trades, not planning surveys.** Their pattern is what buyers are used to, not proof it suits this job.
- **One scrape each.** Pages behind scripts may show less than a person sees.
