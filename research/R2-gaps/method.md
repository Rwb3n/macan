# R2: method (written 2026-10-23, AFTER the read, to record what was asked; this study had no pre-registered rule)

Principal, 2026-10-23: "hmmmm explore gaps".

**R2 was exploration, not a screen.** There was no decision rule, so it was never pre-registered. This file records what each reader was asked, taken from the task text sent to them, so the study can be read alongside its result. Nothing here was written before the evidence was seen.

## Readers
Three parallel sub-agents. Each wrote its own file and every point carries a URL. Firecrawl, United Kingdom; budgets of about 30–35 calls each.

1. **Buyer pain** (`evidence/gap-buyer.md`).
   - **Sources:** forums (Reddit, Mumsnet, MoneySavingExpert, self-build), reviews (Arbtech's especially) and news.
   - **Themes tested:**
     - a missed bat season;
     - finding out at validation that a survey is needed;
     - surprise follow-on costs;
     - slow replies;
     - expired surveys;
     - rejected reports.
   - **Also:** hard numbers, where any exist.
   - **Output:** themes ranked by the number of independent sources, and at most 5 gaps, each a testable guess.
2. **Firm side and price** (`evidence/gap-firms.md`).
   - **Questions:**
     - capacity and season;
     - how small firms win work;
     - published prices for PRA, emergence, PEA, FRA and noise from 8–12 providers;
     - where Arbtech doesn't cover.
   - **Output:** at most 5 gaps.
3. **"What do I need" tools** (`evidence/gap-needs.md`).
   - **Questions:**
     - how 4–5 named councils publish their validation lists;
     - existing checkers;
     - architects as buyers;
     - who runs batsurveys.co.uk and its sister sites (checked at Companies House).
   - **Output:** at most 5 gaps.

## Known weakness
Unlike R1 and R3, the readers' claims were not tied to a column of receipts before the run. That was rule D7, made later. They were asked for a URL for every point, and the result lists the defects.
