# Gap: "which surveys does my application need?" (evidence pass, 2026-10-23)

Question: does anyone help a buyer work out WHICH surveys their planning application needs, before validation fails?
Short answer: **yes, at least one live directory already does it** (Signed Off, `signed-off.co.uk`, launched by a 2024 company), plus council PDFs and survey-firm content marketing. The gap is narrower than assumed. ~35 Firecrawl calls. "Excerpt only" = seen in a search snippet, page not opened.

## 1. Council local validation lists

**National frame (no national survey list):**
- PPG "Making an application": the local list is set by each LPA (para 040), must be "published on its website less than 2 years before an application is submitted" (para 039), "reviewed at least every 2 years" (para 043), and items must be reasonable for the scale of development and material to the decision (para 040). https://www.gov.uk/guidance/making-an-application
- No national bat-survey trigger list is mandated. The de facto standard is the **ALGE (Association of Local Government Ecologists) trigger list**, which councils adapt: Bedford's local list says it "has been adapted from the ALGE template ... Criteria (Trigger List) for When a Survey and Assessment" is needed (excerpt only) https://www.bedford.gov.uk/files/alge-template.pdf/download?inline ; Cornwall's guide refers to "the Association of Local Government Ecologists trigger list" (excerpt only) https://gocollaborate.ams3.digitaloceanspaces.com/gocollaborate-production-spaces/LPG/supporting-information/documents/biodiversity_for_Cornwall_guide.pdf ; BCT's Bat Survey Guidelines (2016) carry "Box 1 Development and planning trigger list for bat surveys" (excerpt only) https://cdn.bats.org.uk/uploads/pdf/Resources/Bat_Survey_Guidelines_2016_NON_PRINTABLE.pdf
- Natural England standing advice, linked by councils: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/protected-species-how-to-review-planning-applications
- The pain has been stated publicly since 2011 by the Planning Portal itself: "the problem of knowing when or if a bat survey will be required ... it has always been something of a mystery as to why survey requirements are not determined nationally rather than locally ... a straightforward national list ... would seem easiest for everyone." https://blog.planningportal.co.uk/2011/11/08/validation-views-from-the-other-side/

A householder has to find their LPA's version and read a criteria table. Machine-readable/national version: **not found**.

## Gaps this suggests (testable guesses)

1. **Signed Off already occupies "checker + directory + quotes".** Guess: its checker is generic (no LPA step), so a postcode → LPA → that council's actual local-list ecology triggers would be a visible differentiator. Test: run the Signed Off checker for a loft conversion and compare its output against the BANES and Rochford triggers for the same project.
2. **Council triggers are ALGE-derived but published in incompatible PDFs.** Guess: for 20 sampled LPAs, ≥70% use an ALGE-style table or yes/no checklist that could be normalised into one dataset. Test: sample 20 local lists, code their bat triggers, measure overlap.
3. **Lists are often out of date relative to the PPG 2-year rule** (e.g. BANES ecology tables 2019/2020; North Yorkshire's unitary list not findable). Guess: ≥30% of sampled LPAs' ecology criteria predate Oct 2024. Test: record the date on each sampled list.
4. **Agents (architects/technologists) are the real chooser and refer informally.** Guess: in 5 calls to small practices, ≥3 say they refer the client to one or two known ecologists and have no formal panel. Test: ask exactly that on S8-style calls.
5. **Survey firms make their own "do I need one" content to capture the same intent** (Arbtech's three keyword sites, ACP's per-council pages). Guess: firms would pay for qualified, validation-triggered leads but not for generic directory listings. Test: ask 5 firms which they would pay for, a listing or a lead pre-tagged with the council trigger that applies.
