# S7: result (2026-10-22)

Run as `method.md` (committed 6d70ea7 before any read). Volumes are in `result.csv`.

## Result

**Passes. Buyer-phrased demand is about 14,800 searches a month,** against the 3,478 bar (S4's figure for "environmental consultant", job seekers included). That is about 4.3× the bar and about 8.9 searches a month per S1 substantive company (1,664).

**Largest terms** (1K–10K band each, midpoint 3,162):
- bat survey
- flood risk assessment
- environmental impact assessment
- environmental consultancy

**Next tier** (100–1K band each, midpoint 316):
- preliminary ecological appraisal
- ecology survey
- phase 1 environmental assessment
- contaminated land survey
- noise impact assessment
- air quality assessment

## How the rule was applied

- **Tool:** Keyword Planner, *Get search volume and forecasts*. UK, Google, Oct 2025 – Sept 2026 (last 12 months), principal's account, run via Browser MCP. The 19 terms went in two batches into one plan; the plan stays in the account (no spend).
- **Merged terms:** Keyword Planner returned 17 rows for the 19 terms. It merged "ecological survey" into "ecology survey" and "environmental consultants" into "environmental consultancy" as close variants. The merged terms count 0, so the total is not double-counted.

## Sensitivity (not the method's result)

The bar still holds when the terms most likely to carry non-buyer traffic are dropped:
- **Without "environmental consultancy" and "environmental impact assessment":** 8,444. Those two are a generic firm search and a term also used by students.
- **Also without "flood risk assessment":** 5,282. That service is often sold by engineering firms under other SICs.

## Defects

- **Coarse bands:** each of the four largest terms is a 1K–10K band read at its 3,162 midpoint. The real total could be several times lower or higher.
- **Shared demand:** flood risk, noise and air quality are also sold by engineering and acoustic firms. The demand isn't exclusive to SIC 74901.
- **One buyer type is untested:** a homeowner needing one bat survey for a planning application counts the same as a developer buying many. Whether these buyers would use a directory, and whether consultancies want their enquiries, is not measured here.
- **The term list is root's,** drafted without volume data but not blind to the hypothesis (stated in the method).
