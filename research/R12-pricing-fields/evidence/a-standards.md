## Defects and limits (reader A)
- **Paywalled British Standards** (BS 5837, BS 4142, BS 10175, BS 42020): only BSI's public pages were read. Their wording is general. For trees, the site-specific fields come from Leeds City Council's restatement of BS 5837 (secondary). BS 42020 appears only as quoted inside CIEEM GPEA (B5 to B7) and BCT GPG.
- **BS 10175:2011+A2:2017 is withdrawn** (28 Jan 2026). The current edition is BS 10175:2026, and I had only BSI's article for it.
- **GCN guidelines receipt:** read via Firecrawl directQuote, not a local text grep. The copy is hosted by National Grid, not Natural England. Section numbers for C8 and C10 are approximate.
- **BCT Table 2.2:** the month bars are graphical. A13 and A14 quote the nearest text instead.
- **HEAN 12:** the extraction lost the start of the "Nature of the Proposal" sentence. F2 quotes the surviving fragment. HEAN 12 rows cite paragraph or section, not page.
