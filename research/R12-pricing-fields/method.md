# R12: what does a surveyor need to know to scope and price each survey? (pre-registered 2026-10-24, before any read)

Principal, 2026-10-24: firms should get a job sheet plus a trimmed excerpt of the buyer's words, not a drafted story (D27). "on the fields a surveyor needs to price a job - surely theres some sort of standard to start from?"

**Use:** the result sets the job sheet's fields, and the questions the buyer is asked, for each survey in the catalogue. Today the catalogue's "what firms need to know" (`ask`) lines rest on no source.

## Surveys, fixed now
The 8 with the most buyer demand or council asks (R2, R8):
1. bat survey;
2. preliminary ecological appraisal;
3. great crested newt survey;
4. flood risk assessment;
5. tree survey (arboricultural);
6. heritage statement;
7. noise impact assessment;
8. land contamination.

The other catalogue surveys inherit fields by group, and that is marked as inference.

## Part A: the standards (one reader)
For each survey, find the published standard or guidance a UK practitioner works to. Leads, unchecked:
- Bat Conservation Trust, *Bat Surveys for Professional Ecologists: Good Practice Guidelines*;
- CIEEM guidance on preliminary ecological appraisal;
- BS 42020;
- Natural England or government guidance on great crested newts;
- gov.uk site-specific flood risk assessment guidance;
- BS 5837;
- Historic England advice notes;
- BS 4142 and ProPG;
- BS 10175, and the Environment Agency's Land Contamination Risk Management.

**Record** what each says the surveyor must know or establish about the site and the proposal before or for the survey: site and building characteristics, the proposed works, timing and season, access, and existing information. Each row carries the document title, edition or date, URL, section and verbatim wording. Paywalled British Standards are recorded from their official summary or scope page only, marked as such.

## Part B: firms' own quote and enquiry forms (one reader)
- **Sample:** 15 UK firms' online quote forms, at least 2 per survey above where they exist. Found by the fixed search "<survey name> quote" UK, taking firms whose site has a quote form, in search order.
- **Record** every field each form asks, verbatim, with the URL. Forms behind a login or that need contact details first are read up to that point only; nothing is submitted.

## Synthesis (Claude)
Each candidate field per survey is marked:
- **core:** in the standard **and** asked by 2 or more firms;
- **useful:** in only one of the two sources;
- **not needed.**

The job sheet uses the core fields, plus the useful fields the buyer can easily answer. Every field carries the rows it rests on.

## Delegation (D7)
- **Receipts:** each reader gives a URL and verbatim text per row.
- **Spot-check:** each reader stops after its first 8 rows for a spot-check.
- **Defects:** rows without a receipt are dropped.

## Limits, stated before the read
- **British Standards are paywalled;** only their public scope wording is used.
- **Quote forms** show what firms ask online, not everything they need, since some is asked later by phone.
- **The other catalogue surveys** inherit by group, marked as inference.
- **Defects** found after the read go in `result.md`; this method is not changed.
