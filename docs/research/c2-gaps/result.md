# C2: gaps (exploration, 2026-10-23)

Principal, 2026-10-23: "hmmmm explore gaps".

**This is exploration, not a screen.** There is no decision rule. It produces guesses to test, each with its test. Three readers worked in parallel, with separate files. Every point in the evidence files carries a URL:
- `evidence/gap-buyer.md`: buyer pain, from forums and reviews;
- `evidence/gap-firms.md`: capacity, how firms win work, and a price table;
- `evidence/gap-needs.md`: council lists, "what do I need" tools, and who runs Arbtech's keyword sites.

## What changed in the picture

1. **Arbtech is bigger than C1 showed.** Its keyword sites (batsurveys.co.uk, ecologysurveys.uk, biodiversitynetgainplan.co.uk) all name Arbtech Consulting Ltd. Arbtech holds 14 of C1's 23 searches, plus #1 for "bat survey near me".
2. **A direct competitor exists that C1 missed: Signed Off** (signed-off.co.uk, operator incorporated November 2024). It runs:
   - a "Which reports do I need?" checker;
   - a directory (75 ecologists);
   - "quotes from up to 3";
   - free and paid featured listings.

   Its checker asks no postcode and no council, so it can't apply the buyer's own council's rules. A second directory, The Planning Review, lists firms found on planning applications.
3. **Bat surveys carry almost all the buyer pain.** Flood, noise, air quality, contaminated land and trees show vendor pages but no buyer complaints.
4. **Biodiversity net gain matters less for small buyers.** Householder applications were already exempt; since 6 August 2026 sites of 0.2 ha or less are too (gov.uk). The survey page is corrected.

## What this suggests (for the principal; the choice is theirs)

The strongest opening is **bats first**, with two promises nobody else makes together:
- **Honest about the season** (G2): we already show it.
- **Independent on the follow-on** (G1): a second quote when phase 2 is recommended.

Both hit the most-cited buyer pains. Both suit local firms against Arbtech's upsell pattern. And G2 gives firms off-season work they lack.

The council-specific checker (G3) is the way to beat Signed Off, but it is data work: 20 local lists to start.

## Defects

- **Arbtech's reviews dominate the evidence.** About 10 of the ~14 sources on the follow-on bill are its 1–2 star Trustpilot reviews. That is one big firm's pattern, not proof about the market.
- **Reddit and Facebook were read as search snippets only.**
- **The Arbtech link is partly inferred.** Linking "Arbtech Consulting Ltd" to arbtech.co.uk rests on the shared name and company number. Arbtech's own pages weren't checked.
- **The price table mixes "from" prices** and unclear scopes (how many visits Arbtech's emergence survey covers is not stated).
- **Search ads are still unmeasured.** That needs Keyword Planner in the principal's account.
